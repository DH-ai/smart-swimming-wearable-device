# 90-second video

Film in this order. The spoken lines are short on purpose. Cut anything that does not show the device, the protractor, the pool, or the result.

Total target: 90 seconds. Export one file, landscape, with the lane visible and the LED in frame when it flashes.

| Time | Picture | Say this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | Swimmer on the block, or a length with no device | "A stroke can feel busy while the entry keeps changing. AquaCue watches the forearm and taps when that wrist angle drifts." |
| 0:08–0:18 | Pod on the forearm, LED toward the camera. Tap the case so it is obvious the sensor does not rattle. | "ESP32, MPU-6050, memory card, vibration motor. The decision is made on the arm. The card keeps the data." |
| 0:18–0:32 | Bench: board on the ruler, serial angle on a laptop, move from flat to about 45°. | "Before the pool, a protractor checks the angle. The gyroscope predicts, the accelerometer corrects, and a still pose sets the zero." |
| 0:32–0:48 | Wall start. LED flash, push-off, two easy strokes where the recovery and the LED can be seen. | "Four easy lengths set the baseline. The first eight strokes stay quiet. They are this swimmer's own normal angle." |
| 0:48–1:05 | Higher-entry lengths. Show the hand clearly higher, then the LED if it fires. | "Then four lengths with a higher hand entry. A cue means two strokes in a row left the baseline. It is a wrist angle, not a claim about fatigue." |
| 1:05–1:18 | Second easy block, then a still of the session table or the script output, with the real numbers. | "Four easy lengths again. We compare the device count to the video, and we compare the angle shift to the scatter in the baseline." |
| 1:18–1:30 | Face or hands holding the pod, pool behind. | "Freestyle only, one session. If the count matches and the cue follows the higher entry, the logger did its job. If it does not, that result stays in the paper." |

Do not read the offline accuracy unless the 90 seconds are already secure. If you add one clause, use: "On short public clips, the gyroscope separated freestyle, and did not separate butterfly from breaststroke."

If the pool has not happened yet, do not film a fake cue or invent a percentage. Film the bench and the device, say the pool protocol in one sentence, and replace 0:32–1:18 after the session.

Checklist before upload:

- The LED flash at the first wall press is in the video.
- The higher hand entry is obvious without a caption.
- Any number spoken aloud is also in `paper/paper.md` and in the CSV.
- The file is at most 90 seconds.
